Care of Jewelry

💍 Protect your jewelry from excessive moisture.

💍 Always remove it before bathing or swimming.

💍 Allow lotions, perfumes, and other sprays to dry completely before wearing your jewelry.

💍 Be sure to remove your jewelry if you need to reapply lotions, perfumes, or other sprays.
